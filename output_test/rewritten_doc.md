# How to Log In to Your OptinMonster Account

In this article, you'll learn how to quickly log in to your **OptinMonster** dashboard to manage your campaigns and settings.

## Logging In to Your OptinMonster Dashboard

To access your account, follow these sequential steps:

1.  Navigate to the official **OptinMonster** website.
2.  Look for the **Log In** button, typically located in the upper right corner of the page, and click it. (See Image 1).
3.  You will be redirected to the login screen. Enter your registered **email** and **password** into the corresponding fields. (See Image 2).
4.  Click the **Submit** button.

## Troubleshooting Login Issues

If you encounter issues while trying to access your account, review the information below.

**Note:** If you are having trouble logging in because you forgot your password, click the **Forgot Password?** link provided on the login screen to reset your credentials.

That's it! You've successfully logged in and can now access your **OptinMonster** dashboard.
