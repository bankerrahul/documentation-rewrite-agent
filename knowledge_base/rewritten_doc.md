# How to Manage and Organize Your Testimonials

Once you've started collecting social proof, the Thrive Ovation dashboard becomes your central hub for organizing, filtering, and moderating your content. In this article, you'll learn how to use statuses to manage your workflow, find specific testimonials using filters, and manage your tags directly from the dashboard.

---

## 1. Understanding Testimonial Statuses

Every testimonial in your library is assigned a status that determines if it can be displayed on your website.

* **Ready for Display:** These testimonials are live and will appear in any display elements or dynamic lists on your site.
* **Awaiting Review:** Testimonials captured via on-page forms begin here. You must review and approve them before they can be displayed.
* **Awaiting Approval:** Used when you've converted a comment into a testimonial and are waiting for the customer to give permission (via the [Customer Approval workflow](https://thrivethemes.com/docs/how-to-configure-email-delivery-and-customer-approval-settings-in-thrive-ovation/)).
* **Rejected:** Testimonials that you have decided not to use. They remain in your database but will never be shown on the front end.

---

## 2. Navigating the Dashboard

The main Thrive Ovation dashboard (found under **WordPress Admin** -> **Thrive Dashboard** -> **Thrive Ovation**) contains everything you need to find and organize your social proof.

### Filtering and Searching

At the top of the testimonial list, you can use filters to narrow down your view:

* **Status/Image Filter:** Filter by specific workflow statuses or by whether testimonials have an author image.
* **Tag Filter:** Use the tag dropdown to find testimonials with specific labels, or use the **"Untagged"** filter to find items that still need organization.
* **Keyword Search:** Use the search bar to find testimonials by name or specific keywords in the title.

### Customizing the View

Click the **Show/Hide** field at the top of the list to customize which columns appear in your dashboard. You can toggle **Tags**, **Type** (how it was collected), and **Status** to create a view that works best for you.

---

## 3. Managing Testimonial Tags

Thrive Ovation does not have a standalone "Tag Manager" screen. Instead, you manage your tags directly from the dashboard and the testimonial editor.

### Managing Tags in Bulk

You can assign tags to multiple testimonials at once from the main dashboard:

1. Select the testimonials you want to organize using the checkboxes on the left.
2. Click the **Tags** icon in the toolbar (tooltip: "Define tags for selected testimonials").
3. In the dropdown that appears, you can search and select existing tags or see which ones are already applied to your selection.

### Managing Individual Tags

When you add a testimonial manually, import one, or edit an existing entry, you will see a **Tags** section in the form:

* **Assign Tags:** Type in the field to select from your list of existing tags.
* **Create New Tags:** If you type a name that doesn't exist yet, Thrive Ovation's autocomplete will allow you to create the new tag on the fly.

---

## 4. Bulk Actions and Individual Edits

Next to each testimonial, you will find options to **Edit**, **Duplicate**, or **Delete** individual items.

If you need to perform actions on multiple items:

1. Use the checkboxes to select the testimonials.
2. Use the **Bulk Actions** dropdown at the top or bottom of the list to change statuses or delete items in one click.
