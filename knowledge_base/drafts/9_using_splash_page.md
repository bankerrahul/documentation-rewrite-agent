# Using the Splash Page in Thrive Quiz Builder

A Splash Page is an optional introductory screen that helps set the stage for your quiz. In this article, you'll learn when to use a Splash Page and how to customize it to increase completion rates.

## What is a Splash Page?

The Splash Page is the first thing a user sees before they answer any questions. Its primary goal is to "hook" the reader, explain what they'll get from the quiz, and provide a clear starting point.

### When to use a Splash Page

* **Context:** If your quiz topic needs explanation.
* **Motivation:** To highlight the benefits of completing the quiz (e.g., "Find your perfect skincare routine in 2 minutes").
* **Branding:** To establish a visual style before the interaction begins.

---

## How to Add and Customize a Splash Page

1. Navigate to your **Thrive Quiz Builder** dashboard.
2. In the **Quiz Structure** area, locate the **Splash Page** card.
3. Click **Manage**.
4. Choose a template that matches your quiz's goal.
5. Navigate to your **Thrive Quiz Builder** dashboard.
6. In the **Quiz Structure** area, locate the **Splash Page** card.
7. Click **Manage**.
8. Choose a template that matches your quiz's goal.
9. Click the **Edit with Thrive Architect** button (or the pencil icon).

### Key Customization Steps

* **Headline:** Use a compelling H1 title that promises a specific outcome.
* **Call to Action (CTA):** Every Splash Page needs a button to start the quiz.
* **Dynamic Linking:** If you add a custom button, make sure it is linked to the **Next step in quiz** (see our guide on [Navigation & Flow](quiz-navigation-progress-bars-flow-control)).
* **Visuals:** Add high-quality images or icons that represent the quiz topic.

## Technical Tips

* **A/B Testing:** You can A/B test different Splash Pages to see which headline or image leads to more quiz starts.
* **Minimalist Design:** If you want users to start immediately, keep the Splash Page simple with just a headline and a large "Start Quiz" button.
