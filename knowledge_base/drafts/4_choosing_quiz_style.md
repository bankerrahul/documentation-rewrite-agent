# How to Choose and Editing Your Quiz Style

The appearance of your quiz is just as important as the content. In this article, you'll learn how to select a visual style for your quiz and how to use the advanced editor to customize colors and typography.

## Step 1: Picking a Unified Quiz Style

Selecting a style ensures that your Splash Page, Questions, Opt-in Gate, and Results Page all share a consistent design.

1. In your quiz dashboard, find the **Quiz Style** card and click **Choose Style**.
2. Browse through the available design templates.
3. Click on a style to see a preview of how the different pages (e.g., questions vs. results) will look.
4. Once you find a design you like, click the **Use this style** button.

---

## Step 2: Customizing the Visual Design

If the default style doesn't perfectly match your brand, you can edit it.

> [!NOTE]
> Granular editing of individual colors and typography is currently most robust in the **Minimalist** style.

1. On the **Quiz Style** card, click the **Pencil Icon** (Edit) or the **Change Style** button.
2. If you are using the **Minimalist** style, you can customize the following:
    * **Colors:** Change the primary accent colors used for buttons, progress bars, and highlights.
    * **Typography:** Set the font family, size, and style for both **Questions** and **Answers** independently.
    * **Layout:** Adjust the spacing and alignment of elements.
3. Click the **Save** or **Apply** buttons as you make changes to see them in the live preview.

---

## Step 3: Resetting to Defaults

If you've made changes and want to start over:

1. Open the style editor via the **Pencil Icon**.
2. Look for the **Reset to Default Layout** option.
3. Confirm the reset to revert all visual customizations to the original template settings.
