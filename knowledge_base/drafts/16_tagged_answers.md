# Building Tagged Answers for Audience Segmentation

Tagged answers allow you to qualify and segment your leads based on every single choice they make in a quiz. In this article, you'll learn how to apply tags to individual answers and how these tags are sent to your email marketing service.

## How to Enable Tagged Answers

You can assign tags to any multiple-choice or audio/video question.

1. In the **Questions Editor**, click the **Pencil Icon** to edit a specific question.
2. In the answer setup area, look for the **Tags** toggle and switch it to **ON**.
3. A **Tags input box** will appear next to each answer option.
4. Type the tags you want to apply when a user selects that answer (e.g., "Interest: SEO", "Budget: High").
5. You can add up to **5 tags per answer**.
6. Click **Save**.

---

## How Tags are Delivered

It is important to understand that tags are only sent to your mailing list if the user completes a lead generation form.

1. **Selection:** The user clicks a tagged answer during the quiz.
2. **Tracking:** Thrive Quiz Builder "remembers" these tags as the user progresses.
3. **Submission:** When the user enters their email on the **Opt-in Gate** or **Results Page**, all the tags they "earned" during the quiz are sent to your connected email marketing service.

---

## Strategies for Segmentation

* **Interest-Based Tagging:** Tag answers that reveal a user's specific pain points or interests (e.g., "Weight Loss" vs. "Muscle Gain").
* **Buyer Intent:** Tag answers that indicate how ready a user is to buy (e.g., "Researching" vs. "Ready to start now").
* **Demographics:** Tag answers that clarify the user's role or business size.

> [!TIP]
> Use these tags in your CRM to trigger specific **Automation Sequences**. For example, a user tagged with "Budget: High" could be added to a VIP sales pipeline, while someone tagged with "Researching" receives a series of educational blog posts.
