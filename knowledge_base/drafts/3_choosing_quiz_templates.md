# How to Choose and Customize Quiz Templates

Starting with a template is the fastest way to build an effective quiz. In this article, you'll learn about the three goal-oriented templates available in Thrive Quiz Builder and how to customize them for your needs.

## Choosing a Template

When you click **Add New** in the Thrive Quiz Builder dashboard, you are presented with four options. While **Build from scratch** gives you a blank canvas, the other three are pre-configured to help you achieve specific business goals.

---

## 1. The List Building Template

Use this template if your primary goal is to grow your email list.

* **Pre-configured Workflow:** Automatically sets up a **Splash Page**, **Questions**, an **Opt-in Gate**, and a **Results Page**.
* **Unique Feature:** The **Opt-in Gate** is mandatory. This means users must enter their email address after finishing the questions to see their results.
* **Best for:** Creating "lead magnet" quizzes where the value is in the personalized result.

---

## 2. The Social Share Template

Use this template to maximize brand awareness through viral sharing.

* **Pre-configured Workflow:** Sets up a **Splash Page**, **Questions**, and a **Results Page**.
* **Unique Feature:** The **Results Page** is pre-filled with **Social Share Badges**. These badges are designed to look great on social feeds and include a clear call to action for the user to share their outcome.
* **Best for:** "Which [Character/Type] are you?" style quizzes that are fun and engaging.

---

## 3. The Gain Customer Insights Template

Use this template for market research and gathering qualitative data.

* **Pre-configured Workflow:** Sets up a **Splash Page**, **Questions**, and a **Results Page**.
* **Unique Feature:** This template omits the Opt-in Gate by default to reduce friction. It encourages the use of **Open-Ended Questions** and **Multiple Choice** answers to help you learn more about your audience's preferences and pain points.
* **Best for:** Customer feedback surveys, product preference polls, and audience segmentation.

---

## How to Customize Your Template

Once you've chosen a template, you can customize every part of it:

1. **Add Your Content:** Navigate to the **Questions** card and click **Manage** to swap out the placeholder questions with your own.
2. **Design the Pages:** Click **Manage** on the **Splash Page**, **Opt-in Gate**, or **Results Page** cards to open the Thrive Architect editor and change the text, colors, and layout.
3. **Add Your Content:** Navigate to the **Questions** card and click **Manage** to swap out the placeholder questions with your own.
4. **Design the Pages:** Click **Manage** on the **Splash Page**, **Opt-in Gate**, or **Results Page** cards to open the Thrive Architect editor and change the text, colors, and layout.
5. **Adjust the Evaluation:** Go to the **Quiz Type** card to change how the scores or categories are calculated (see our [Guide to Quiz Types](URL)).
