# How to Create and Use Form Templates

In this article, you'll learn how to save your form designs as templates to reuse them across different Lead Groups and campaigns.

## Saving a Custom Template

Once you have designed a form exactly how you want it, you can save it for future use.

1. Open your form in **Thrive Architect**.
2. In the right sidebar, click the **Gear Icon** (Settings).
3. Choose **Save as Template**.
4. Enter a name for your template (e.g., "My Brand Lightbox").
5. Click **Save**.

## Loading a Template

When creating a new form, you can load your saved designs.

1. Create a new form in your Lead Group.
2. When the editor opens, a template library popup usually appears.
3. Filter by **"Saved Templates"** (or look for the **My Templates** tab).
4. Select your template.

If you are already in the editor:

1. Click the **Cloud Icon** (Change Template) in the right sidebar.
2. **Warning:** This will replace your current design entirely.
3. Select your saved template from the library.

## Exporting and Importing Templates

You can move templates between websites.

1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Leads**.
2. Click the **Gear Icon** (Settings) in the top right.
3. Select **Export Templates**.
4. Choose the templates you want to export. This will download a `.zip` file.
5. On the other site, go to the same menu and select **Import Templates**.
6. Upload the `.zip` file.

That's it! You can now reuse your high-converting designs anywhere.
