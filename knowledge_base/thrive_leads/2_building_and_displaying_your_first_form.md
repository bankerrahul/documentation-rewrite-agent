# How to Build and Display Your First Form

Ready to start collecting leads? This guide will walk you through creating your first opt-in form using the **Lead Groups** method, which is the most common and powerful way to use Thrive Leads.

## Step 1: Create a Lead Group

1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard >> Thrive Leads**.
2. In the "Lead Groups" section, click **Add New**.
3. Name your group (e.g., "Main Newsletter") and click **Add Lead Group**.

## Step 2: Define Display Settings

You need to tell Thrive Leads *where* to show this form.

1. Click the **Cogwheel Icon** (Mobile/Display Settings) next to your new Lead Group.
2. A popup will appear listing all your site's content.
3. Select where you want the form to appear. For a simple start, check **"All Posts"**.
4. Click **Save & Close**.

## Step 3: Create a Form Type

Now you need to choose *what kind* of form to show.

1. Click the **Add New Type of Opt-In Form** button inside your Lead Group.
2. Choose **"Lightbox"** (a popup) for this example.
3. The "Lightbox" card will appear in your Lead Group.

## Step 4: Add and Design the Form

1. Click the **Add** button (or the **Plus** icon) on the Lightbox card.
2. Click **Create Form**.
3. Name your form (e.g., "Default Design") and click **Create Form**.
4. Click the **Edit Design** (pencil icon) button.
5. **Thrive Architect** will open relative to your form.
6. A template library will pop up. Browse and select a template that suits your goal.
7. Customize the text, colors, and images using the visual editor.
8. **Important**: Click on the "Lead Generation" element (the form fields) and configure your connection to your email service (e.g., Mailchimp) in the left sidebar under "Connection".
9. Click **Save Work** in the bottom left corner and close the editor tab.

## Step 5: Configure Triggers and Display Frequency

Back in the Thrive Leads Dashboard, look at your form stats. You will see settings for "Trigger" and "Display Frequency".

### Trigger

Defines *when* the form appears.

1. Click on the default trigger (usually "Displays immediately on page load").
2. Change it to something less intrusive if desired, like "Show after a certain period of time" (e.g., 10 seconds) or "Show when the user scrolls to a percentage of the way down the content" (e.g., 50%).
3. Click **Save**.

### Display Frequency

Defines how often a visitor sees the form.

1. Click on the default setting (e.g., "Every 7 days").
2. Adjust the slider. Set it to **0** if you want it to show *every time* (good for testing), or leave it at 7-30 days to avoid annoying repeat visitors.
3. Click **Save**.

## Step 6: Activate Your Form

1. On the form design line, toggle the switch on the far right relative to "Desktop" (and Mobile) to **ON**.
2. Go back to the main Thrive Leads Dashboard.
3. Verify that your **Lead Group** is also toggled to **ON** (green).

Congratulations! Your form is now live on all your blog posts. Visit a post in an Incognito/Private window to test it out.

## Alternative: Using Shortcodes or ThriveBoxes

If you prefer not to use automatic placement, you can create a **Lead Shortcode** or **ThriveBox**.

1. Scroll down to the "Lead Shortcodes" or "ThriveBoxes" section in the dashboard.
2. Click **Add New** and follow the same design steps as above.
3. Once designed, copy the **Shortcode** displayed next to the form title.
4. Paste that shortcode into any WordPress post or page content.
