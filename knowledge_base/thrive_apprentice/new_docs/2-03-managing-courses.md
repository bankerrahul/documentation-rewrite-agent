# How to Manage Course Status and Bulk Actions
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to manage course statuses in Thrive Apprentice—including publishing, hiding, archiving, and scheduling courses—as well as how to use bulk actions to manage multiple courses at once.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Understanding Course Status Options
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Every course in Thrive Apprentice has a status that controls its visibility and accessibility. Here are the available statuses:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Published** — The course is live and visible to all visitors on your site. Students with access can enroll and start learning.
- **Draft** — The course is not visible to anyone except administrators. Use this status while you're still building or editing your course content.
- **Hidden** — The course is not listed publicly, but users with a direct link can still access it. This is ideal for exclusive or invite-only courses.
- **Archived** — The course is removed from public listings and is no longer accessible to new students. Existing students who previously had access may still see it depending on your configuration.
- **Scheduled** — The course is set to publish automatically at a specific date and time in the future.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## How to Change a Course's Status
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click **Courses** in the left sidebar to view your course list.
3. Hover over the course you want to modify.
4. Click the **status indicator** or select **Edit** to open the course settings.
5. Locate the **Status** dropdown and choose the desired status: **Published**, **Draft**, **Hidden**, **Archived**, or **Scheduled**.
6. Click **Save** to apply the change.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** Changing a course from **Published** to **Draft** or **Archived** immediately removes it from public view. Students currently in the middle of a lesson won't be interrupted, but they won't be able to navigate to the course from the course listing page.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Scheduling a Course for Future Publication
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The scheduling feature lets you set a course—or individual modules and lessons—to publish automatically at a specific date and time. This is perfect for drip content strategies or coordinated launch events.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice** and open the course you want to schedule.
2. Click the **Content** tab to view all modules and lessons.
3. Hover over the **Publish** icon next to the lesson or module you want to schedule.
4. Select **Schedule** from the options that appear.
5. Set the **date** and **time** you want the content to go live.
6. Click **Save** to confirm the schedule.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once the scheduled date arrives, Thrive Apprentice automatically publishes the content—no manual action required on your part.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Scheduling works at both the course and lesson level. You can publish a course immediately while scheduling individual lessons to release over time—a great approach for drip-feeding content to your students.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Hiding Premium Courses from the Course List
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If you offer premium courses that should only be visible to specific users or members, you can hide them from the public course list. This keeps your premium content exclusive while still allowing authorized users to access it via a direct link or through their member dashboard.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice** > **Courses**.
2. Locate the premium course you want to hide.
3. Change the course status to **Hidden**.
4. Share the direct course URL only with authorized members or use your product access settings to control visibility.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Combine the **Hidden** status with product-level access restrictions for maximum control. Students who purchase or are granted access to the product will still see the course in their dashboard, even though it's hidden from the public listing.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Using Bulk Actions to Manage Multiple Courses
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
When you need to make changes to several courses at once, bulk actions save you significant time. Instead of editing each course individually, you can select multiple courses and apply changes in a single operation.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### How to Use Bulk Actions
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Open the course you want to manage and click the **Content** tab.
3. Select the checkboxes next to the lessons, modules, or chapters you want to modify.
4. A **Bulk Actions** toolbar appears at the top of the content list.
5. Choose the action you want to apply from the toolbar options.
6. Confirm the action to apply it to all selected items.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Available Bulk Actions
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Move** — Relocate selected lessons or modules to a different chapter or position within the course.
- **Duplicate** — Create copies of selected lessons or modules.
- **Delete** — Remove selected items from the course.
- **Change status** — Publish, unpublish, or schedule multiple items at once.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** Bulk delete is permanent. Double-check your selections before confirming a bulk delete action to avoid accidentally removing content you still need.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Conclusion
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully learned how to manage course statuses, schedule future publications, hide premium courses, and use bulk actions to streamline your workflow. These tools give you complete control over how and when your course content appears to students.
