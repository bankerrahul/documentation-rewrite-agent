# How to Create a Free Course Funnel in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to create a free online course funnel using Thrive Apprentice and Thrive Architect—a proven strategy for building your email list by offering a free course as a lead generation tool.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

## What Is a Free Course Funnel?
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
A free course funnel is a marketing strategy where you offer a free online course in exchange for a visitor's email address. Instead of selling the course directly, you use it as a lead magnet—attracting potential customers, growing your email list, and nurturing them toward purchasing your paid products or services.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Why Use a Free Course Funnel?
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Free course funnels are one of the most effective ways to grow your audience because:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **High perceived value** — A free course feels more valuable than a simple PDF download, which increases opt-in rates.
- **Email list building** — You collect email addresses from every student who enrolls, giving you a direct line of communication.
- **Trust building** — Delivering quality content for free demonstrates your expertise and builds trust with your audience.
- **Upsell opportunities** — Once students complete the free course, you can promote your paid courses, memberships, or services.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before you begin, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Thrive Apprentice** installed and activated on your WordPress site.
- **Thrive Architect** installed for building your landing page.
- An **email marketing service** connected to your site (e.g., Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, ConvertKit).
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Step 1: Create Your Free Course in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click the **Courses** tab in the left sidebar.
3. Click **Add Course** and enter a compelling course title.
4. Add a course description that highlights what students will learn.
5. Add your lessons with the free content you want to deliver.
6. Set the **Course Status** to **Published**.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Keep your free course focused and concise—three to five lessons work well. You want to deliver enough value to impress your audience without giving away everything you have to offer.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Step 2: Build a Landing Page with Thrive Architect
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Create a new page in WordPress (e.g., "Free Course: [Your Topic]").
2. Click **Launch Thrive Architect** to open the visual editor.
3. Choose a landing page template or start from scratch.
4. Add a headline that clearly states the benefit of the free course.
5. Include bullet points listing what students will learn.
6. Add social proof elements if available (testimonials, student count, etc.).
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Step 3: Configure Access—No Product or Payment Needed
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Since this is a free course, you don't need to create a product or connect a payment gateway. You simply need to ensure students can access the course after providing their email:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In Thrive Apprentice, make sure the course is not linked to any paid product.
2. Set the course access to allow any logged-in user to view the content.
3. New students will be created as WordPress users when they opt in through your form.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Step 4: Set Up the Opt-In Form
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In Thrive Architect, add a **Lead Generation** element to your landing page.
2. Connect the form to your email marketing service using the **API Connection** settings.
3. Configure the form fields—at minimum, collect the student's **Name** and **Email Address**.
4. Set the **After Submission** action to redirect students to the course page or a thank-you page with a link to the course.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** Make sure the opt-in form creates a WordPress user account so the student can log in and access the course content in Thrive Apprentice.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Step 5: Connect Your Email Service
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **API Connections**.
2. Click **Add New Connection** and select your email service provider.
3. Enter your API key or credentials and click **Connect**.
4. Return to your landing page and link the opt-in form to the appropriate email list or tag.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Step 6: Test the Funnel
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before promoting your funnel, test every step of the process:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Visit your landing page as a logged-out user.
2. Submit the opt-in form with a test email address.
3. Confirm you receive the email in your email marketing service.
4. Verify the redirect takes you to the course or thank-you page.
5. Log in as the test user and confirm you can access all course lessons.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** Test from an incognito or private browser window to simulate a first-time visitor's experience accurately.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully created a free course funnel that captures leads, grows your email list, and delivers valuable content to your new subscribers automatically.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
