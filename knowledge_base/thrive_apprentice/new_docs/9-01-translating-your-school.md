# How to Translate Your Thrive Apprentice Online School
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to translate your Thrive Apprentice online school into other languages—using built-in translation features, the Thrive Architect checkout form translator, and the "Say What" plugin for deeper string customization.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Understanding Translation Options
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Thrive Apprentice offers multiple ways to translate your school depending on what you need to change:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Built-in Labels & Translations** — Translate default UI labels (buttons, messages, navigation) directly from Thrive Apprentice settings.
- **Thrive Architect Checkout Forms** — Translate checkout form fields and button text within the visual editor.
- **"Say What" Plugin** — Override specific plugin and theme strings that aren't editable through the built-in settings.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Translating Labels in Thrive Apprentice Settings
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The fastest way to translate your school's interface is through the built-in **Labels & Translations** section.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
3. Select the **Labels & Translations** tab.
4. You'll see a list of all default text strings used across your school—button labels, progress messages, navigation text, and access restriction messages.
5. Click on any label to edit it.
6. Enter your translated text in the field.
7. Click **Save** when you're done.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Use the search bar at the top of the labels list to quickly find specific strings like "Start Course," "Next Lesson," or "Complete."
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Translating Checkout Forms in Thrive Architect
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If you're selling courses through Thrive Apprentice's built-in checkout, you can translate the checkout form directly in **Thrive Architect**.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Open the page containing your checkout form in **Thrive Architect**.
2. Click on the **Checkout** element to select it.
3. In the left sidebar, look for the text fields—field labels, placeholder text, button text, and error messages.
4. Replace each English string with your translated version.
5. Click **Save Work** when finished.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** Checkout form translations are page-specific. If you have multiple checkout pages, you'll need to translate each one individually.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Using the "Say What" Plugin for Advanced Translations
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Some strings are hardcoded in the plugin or theme and can't be changed through the settings panel. The free **Say What** plugin lets you override these strings without editing code.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Install and activate the **Say What** plugin from the WordPress plugin repository.
2. Go to **Tools** > **Text Changes** in your WordPress dashboard.
3. Click **Add New**.
4. In the **Original String** field, enter the exact English text you want to replace.
5. In the **Text Domain** field, enter `thrive-apprentice` (or the relevant plugin's text domain).
6. In the **Replacement String** field, enter your translated text.
7. Click **Save Changes**.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** The original string must match exactly—including capitalization and punctuation—for the replacement to work. If a replacement isn't showing, double-check the string and text domain.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Best Practices for Multilingual Course Sites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Start with built-in labels** — The Labels & Translations section covers most student-facing text and should be your first stop.
- **Test as a student** — Log out or use an incognito window to verify translations appear correctly across course pages, lesson navigation, and checkout.
- **Keep translations consistent** — Use the same terminology across labels, checkout forms, and email templates for a professional experience.
- **Consider a multilingual plugin** — For fully multilingual sites (multiple language versions), consider plugins like **WPML** or **TranslatePress** alongside Thrive Apprentice's built-in features.
<!-- /wp:list -->
