# How to Attach Downloadable Files to Lessons in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to attach downloadable files—such as PDFs, worksheets, checklists, and other resources—to your Thrive Apprentice lessons so students can access supplementary materials directly from the lesson page.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Why Attach Downloadable Files?
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Downloadable resources enhance the learning experience by providing students with materials they can reference offline. Common examples include:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- PDF guides and workbooks
- Worksheets and checklists
- Slide decks and presentations
- Code files or templates
- Certificates of completion
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Attaching Files Using the Resources Feature
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Thrive Apprentice provides a built-in resources feature that lets you attach files directly to any lesson.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click **Courses** in the left sidebar and open the course containing your lesson.
3. Click on the lesson where you want to attach a file.
4. In the lesson settings panel, locate the **Resources** section.
5. Click the **Add Resources** button.
6. Choose **Upload File** to select a file from your computer, or **Media Library** to choose a file you've already uploaded.
7. Once the file is uploaded, give it a descriptive label (e.g., "Module 1 Worksheet" or "Lesson Checklist").
8. Click **Save** to attach the file to the lesson.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Use clear, descriptive file names and labels so students immediately understand what each download contains.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Adding Download Links Within Lesson Content
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can also embed download links directly inside your lesson content for more contextual placement.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Open the lesson and click **Edit Content** to launch **Thrive Architect**.
2. Add a **Text** element or **Button** element where you want the download link.
3. Highlight the text or configure the button, then click the **Link** icon.
4. Upload or select your file from the **Media Library** and insert its URL.
5. Click **Save Work**, then click **Done**.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
This approach works well when you want to offer a download at a specific point in the lesson—for example, a diploma or certificate link at the end of a final lesson.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Where Students See the Downloads
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Files attached via the **Resources** section appear in a dedicated resources area on the lesson page. The exact placement depends on your lesson template, but typically resources display below or beside the main lesson content. Students can click the file name or a download icon to save the file to their device.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** Only students who have access to the lesson can see and download attached files. If a lesson is restricted, the resources remain hidden from unauthorized visitors.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Managing Attached Files
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can update or remove attached files at any time.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **To replace a file:** Remove the existing file from the **Resources** section and upload the updated version.
- **To delete a file:** Click the **Remove** icon next to the file in the **Resources** section, then click **Save**.
- **To reorder files:** Drag and drop the files within the **Resources** section to change their display order.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** If you delete a file from your WordPress **Media Library**, it will also be removed from any lessons where it was attached. Make sure you no longer need a file before deleting it from the library.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully attached downloadable files to your Thrive Apprentice lessons, giving students easy access to supplementary materials that support their learning.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
