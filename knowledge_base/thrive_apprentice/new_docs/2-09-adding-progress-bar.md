# How to Add a Progress Bar to Your Thrive Apprentice Lessons
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to add a progress bar to your Thrive Apprentice lesson templates—giving students a clear visual indicator of how far they've advanced through your course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## What Does the Progress Bar Show?
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The progress bar displays a student's completion percentage for the current course. As learners finish each lesson, the bar fills up accordingly, providing instant visual feedback on their overall advancement. This keeps students motivated and helps them understand how much content remains.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Adding the Progress Bar to a Lesson Template
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The progress bar is added through **Thrive Architect**, the visual editor used to design your lesson layouts. You can add it to individual lessons or—more efficiently—to your lesson template so it appears on every lesson automatically.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Adding to a Lesson Template (Recommended)
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click **Design** in the left sidebar to access your course templates.
3. Select the lesson template you want to edit and click **Edit with Thrive Architect**.
4. In the Thrive Architect editor, click the **Add Element** button (the plus icon) in the right sidebar.
5. Search for **Progress Bar** in the element list.
6. Drag the **Progress Bar** element to your desired location within the lesson layout.
7. Click **Save Work** when you're finished, then click **Done** to return to Thrive Apprentice.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Placing the progress bar near the top of your lesson template—above or below the lesson title—ensures students see it immediately when they open any lesson.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Adding to an Individual Lesson
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Navigate to the course and open the lesson you want to edit.
3. Click **Edit Content** to open the lesson in **Thrive Architect**.
4. Click the **Add Element** button and search for **Progress Bar**.
5. Drag it into your lesson content area.
6. Click **Save Work**, then click **Done**.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Customization Options
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once you've placed the progress bar, click on it to reveal its customization options in the left sidebar panel. You can adjust:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Bar Color** — Change the fill color to match your brand or course theme.
- **Background Color** — Set the color of the unfilled portion of the bar.
- **Height and Width** — Adjust the bar's dimensions to fit your layout.
- **Border Radius** — Round the corners for a softer appearance.
- **Label Display** — Show or hide the percentage label on the bar.
- **Typography** — Customize the font, size, and color of the percentage text.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** The progress bar automatically calculates completion based on the number of lessons a student has marked as complete within the course. You don't need to configure the calculation logic—it works out of the box.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Where the Progress Bar Appears
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If you add the progress bar to your lesson template, it will appear on every lesson that uses that template. If you add it to an individual lesson's content, it will only appear on that specific lesson. For a consistent student experience, the template approach is recommended.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully added a progress bar to your Thrive Apprentice lessons, helping students track their journey through your course at a glance.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
