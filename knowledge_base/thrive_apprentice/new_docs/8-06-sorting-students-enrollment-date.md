# How to Sort Students by Enrollment Date in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to sort and filter your student list by enrollment date in the Thrive Apprentice members section—helping you quickly identify recent enrollments, track cohorts, and manage your student base.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before you begin, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Thrive Apprentice** installed and activated on your WordPress site.
- At least one course with enrolled students.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Accessing the Members List
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In your WordPress dashboard, navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click the **Members** tab in the left sidebar.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The members list shows all registered students along with their enrollment details.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Sorting Students by Enrollment Date
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can sort the student list to display members in order of when they enrolled.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Members** list, locate the column headers at the top of the table.
2. Click on the **Enrollment Date** column header to sort by date.
3. Click once to sort in **ascending order** (oldest enrollments first).
4. Click again to switch to **descending order** (newest enrollments first).
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Sorting by newest first is the most useful view when you want to check who recently joined your courses or verify that new enrollments are coming in as expected.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Filtering by Date Range
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If you need to view students who enrolled during a specific time period, you can use the date range filter.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. At the top of the **Members** list, look for the filter controls.
2. Select the **Date Range** filter.
3. Choose a **Start Date** and **End Date** to define the enrollment window.
4. The list will update to show only students who enrolled within the selected time frame.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** You can combine the date range filter with other filters, such as filtering by a specific course. This allows you to see all students who enrolled in a particular course during a given month, for example.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Filtering by Course
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
To narrow down the list even further, you can filter students by a specific course alongside the enrollment date sort.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Use the **Course** filter dropdown to select the course you want to view.
2. The list updates to show only students enrolled in that course.
3. With the course filter active, click the **Enrollment Date** column header to sort those students by their enrollment date.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Practical Use Cases
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Here are some common scenarios where sorting by enrollment date is helpful:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Tracking Launch Results** — After launching a new course, sort by newest enrollment date to see sign-ups rolling in and monitor early adoption.
- **Managing Cohorts** — If you run cohort-based courses, filter by the enrollment date range that corresponds to each cohort to view those specific students.
- **Identifying Inactive Students** — Sort by oldest enrollment date and cross-reference with progress data to find students who enrolled long ago but haven't made progress.
- **Support and Onboarding** — View recent enrollees to proactively reach out with welcome messages or onboarding resources.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully learned how to sort and filter students by enrollment date in Thrive Apprentice, making it easy to manage and monitor your student base.
