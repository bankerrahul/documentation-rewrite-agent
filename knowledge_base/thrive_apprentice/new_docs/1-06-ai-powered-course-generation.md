# How to Use AI Course Generation in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to use Thrive Apprentice's AI Course Generation features to build and launch courses in minutes—whether you're starting from scratch or remixing existing content.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The **AI Course Generation** tool helps you overcome content creation paralysis by transforming you from a writer into an Editor-in-Chief. Instead of staring at a blank page, you let the AI handle the heavy lifting while you focus on adding your expertise and personal touch.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before you begin, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Thrive Apprentice** installed and activated on your website.
- An active internet connection to access the AI generation services.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Generating a Course from a Prompt ("Prompt-to-Course")
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The **Prompt-to-Course** engine allows you to generate a full course structure—including titles, modules, lesson headers, and content—from a simple text description.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click the **Courses** tab.
3. Click the **Add Course** button.
4. Select **Generate with AI**.
5. In the **Topic** field, enter a brief description of what you want to teach (e.g., "Beginner's Guide to Urban Gardening").
6. Adjust the **Difficulty** and **Course Length** settings to match your target audience.
7. Click **Generate Course Structure**.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The AI will create a proposed outline for your course. From here:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Review the generated outline carefully.
2. Regenerate specific sections or edit titles as needed.
3. Once you are satisfied with the structure, click **Generate Content** to have the AI write the lesson summaries and text.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** The more specific your topic description, the better the AI output. Instead of "marketing," try "email marketing strategies for small e-commerce businesses."
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Remixing an Existing Course
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The **Course Remixer** allows you to take a course you've already built and use it as a seed to create a new, fully written course for a different niche.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Find the course you want to use as a template in the **Courses** list.
3. Hover over the course and select the **Remix** option.
4. Enter the **New Topic** you want to adapt the content for (e.g., changing "Social Media for Realtors" to "Social Media for Dentists").
5. The AI will rewrite the content, keeping the proven structure of your original course but adapting the terminology and examples for the new audience.
6. Review the changes and click **Save** to create your new course.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** The remix feature preserves your original course—it creates a separate copy with the new content, so your existing course remains untouched.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Refining Your Course (Editor's Control)
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You always maintain full control over AI-generated content. You are the Editor-in-Chief; the AI is your ghostwriter. Here's how to refine the output:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Regenerate Sections** — If a specific lesson doesn't sound right, click the **Regenerate** button to get a fresh version.
- **Tweak Tone** — Adjust the detailed settings to make the content sound more formal, casual, or enthusiastic.
- **Manual Edits** — Edit any text field directly, just like you would in a regular WordPress post. This allows you to add your unique voice, stories, and expertise to the draft provided by the AI.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** Think of AI-generated content as a first draft that handles roughly 80% of the work. You should always step in for the final 20%—adding your unique anecdotes, real-world examples, and personal expertise. It is much faster to edit a solid draft than to write from a blank page.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Frequently Asked Questions
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Will the content sound robotic?**
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The AI produces a solid first draft, but it works best when you treat it as a starting point. Add your personal voice, swap in your own examples, and adjust the tone to match your brand. The result is content that sounds like you—created in a fraction of the time.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Is using AI for course creation "cheating"?**
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Not at all—it's efficiency. Just like using a calculator for math, using AI for drafting handles the repetitive structural work so you can focus on delivering high-value expertise and insights that only you can provide.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully learned how to use AI Course Generation to create courses from a prompt, remix existing courses for new audiences, and refine AI-generated content with your own expertise.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
