# How to View Member Related Data in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to view individual member data in Thrive Apprentice—including course enrollment status, progress tracking, completion history, and member activity details.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before you begin, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Thrive Apprentice** installed and activated on your WordPress site.
- At least one course with enrolled students.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Accessing the Members Section
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In your WordPress dashboard, navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click the **Members** tab in the left sidebar.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The Members section displays a list of all registered users who have interacted with your courses. From here, you can search for specific members, browse the full list, and click into individual profiles for detailed information.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Viewing the Members List
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The main members list gives you a high-level overview of your student base.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Name and Email** — Each entry shows the member's display name and email address for easy identification.
- **Enrolled Courses** — A summary of how many courses the member is enrolled in.
- **Overall Progress** — A quick indicator of the member's progress across their enrolled courses.
- **Status** — Shows whether the member is active, has completed courses, or has been inactive.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Use the search bar at the top of the members list to quickly find a specific student by name or email address.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Viewing an Individual Member's Profile
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
To see detailed data for a specific student:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Members** list, click on the student's name to open their profile.
2. The member profile page displays all relevant data for that student.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The profile is organized into several areas of information:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Course Enrollment Status
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
This section shows every course the member is enrolled in, along with:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Course Name** — The title of each enrolled course.
- **Enrollment Date** — When the student was granted access to the course.
- **Access Type** — How the student gained access (e.g., through a product purchase, manual enrollment, or free access).
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Progress Tracking
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
For each enrolled course, you can see the student's detailed progress:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Completion Percentage** — A visual progress bar showing how much of the course the student has finished.
- **Lessons Completed** — A count and list of completed lessons.
- **Current Lesson** — The lesson the student last accessed or is currently working on.
- **Modules Completed** — If the course uses modules, you can see which modules are fully completed.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Completion History
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If the student has finished one or more courses, the completion history section displays:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Completed Courses** — A list of all courses the student has finished.
- **Completion Dates** — The date each course was completed.
- **Certificates** — If certificates are enabled, you can see whether the student has a certificate available and access it directly.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Member Activity
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The activity section provides a timeline of the student's interactions:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Recent Actions** — A chronological log of lesson views, lesson completions, and course completions.
- **Last Active Date** — When the student last accessed any course content.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Searching and Filtering Members
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
When you have a large number of students, searching and filtering helps you find the information you need quickly.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Use the **Search** bar at the top of the Members list to find students by name or email.
2. Use the available filters to narrow the list:
   - **Course** — Show only members enrolled in a specific course.
   - **Status** — Filter by active, completed, or inactive members.
   - **Date Range** — View members who enrolled within a specific time period.
3. The list updates automatically as you apply filters.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** Filters can be combined. For example, you can filter for all active members enrolled in a specific course who joined in the last month.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully learned how to view member-related data in Thrive Apprentice, giving you full visibility into each student's enrollment, progress, and activity.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
