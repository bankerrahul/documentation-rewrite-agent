# How to Enable Course-Level Grading in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to enable and configure course-level grading in Thrive Apprentice—so you can assign overall grades to students based on their performance across multiple assessments within a course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## What Is Course-Level Grading?
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Course-level grading calculates a single final grade for each student by combining their scores from all assessments in a course. Instead of viewing each quiz or assessment result in isolation, students receive an overall course grade that reflects their total performance. This is especially useful for courses that include multiple graded activities like quizzes, assignments, or final exams.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Enabling Course-Level Grading
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click **Courses** in the left sidebar and open the course you want to grade.
3. In the course settings, locate the **Grading** section.
4. Toggle the **Enable Overall Grade** switch to the on position.
5. Click **Save** to apply the change.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** You must have at least one assessment (e.g., a quiz) added to your course before enabling grading. If no assessments exist, the grading option may not appear.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Configuring Grading Options
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once grading is enabled, you can fine-tune how the overall grade is calculated.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Assessment Weighting
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
By default, all assessments contribute equally to the final grade. If certain assessments should count more than others, you can configure individual weights.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Grading** section, click **Set Assessment Weighting**.
2. Assign a weight percentage to each assessment (e.g., midterm quiz at 30%, final exam at 70%).
3. Ensure the total weight adds up to 100%.
4. Click **Save** to confirm your settings.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Use higher weights for comprehensive assessments like final exams, and lower weights for shorter quizzes or checkpoint activities.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Grade Display Settings
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can control how grades are presented to students.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Percentage** — Display the grade as a percentage (e.g., 85%).
- **Letter Grade** — Display the grade as a letter (e.g., A, B, C) based on thresholds you define.
- **Pass/Fail** — Display a simple pass or fail result based on a minimum score threshold.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## How Grades Are Calculated
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Thrive Apprentice considers all completed assessments within the course and calculates the overall grade automatically. If you've configured assessment weighting, each score is multiplied by its assigned weight before being combined. If no custom weights are set, all assessments are averaged equally.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** Only completed assessments are factored into the grade. If a student has not yet attempted an assessment, it will not count toward their overall grade until they submit it.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Where Students See Their Grades
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Students can view their overall course grade in the following locations:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Course Completion Page** — The final grade is displayed on the course completion page after the student finishes all required content.
- **Student Dashboard** — If you've configured a student dashboard, grades appear alongside course progress information.
- **Certificates** — You can issue certificates that include the student's grade automatically. Enable this under the **Certificates** settings for the course.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Combine grading with certificates to reward students who meet a minimum grade threshold—for example, issuing a certificate only to students who score 80% or higher.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully enabled course-level grading in Thrive Apprentice, giving your students clear feedback on their overall performance and adding a professional layer to your online courses.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
