# How to Create Course Bundles in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to create course bundles in Thrive Apprentice—allowing you to package multiple courses together and sell them at a single price point using the Products framework.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## What Are Course Bundles?
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
A course bundle is a collection of two or more courses grouped together and sold as a single product. Bundles let you offer more value to your students at a discounted rate while increasing your revenue per transaction.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
For example, if you sell three individual courses at $25 each, you could bundle all three together and offer the package for $60—giving students a $15 discount while earning more per sale than a single course purchase.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Understanding the Products Framework
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In Thrive Apprentice, bundles are created through the **Products** section. A product is the mechanism that protects and sells your content. Products extend beyond just courses—you can use them to bundle courses with pages, posts, and custom content, making it possible to build entire membership websites.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** Every piece of content you want to restrict or sell needs to be associated with a product. Think of products as the "access keys" your students purchase to unlock content.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before you begin, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Thrive Apprentice** installed and activated on your WordPress site.
- At least **two published courses** ready to bundle together.
- A payment integration configured if you plan to sell the bundle (e.g., SendOwl, WooCommerce, or ThriveCart).
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Creating a Course Bundle Step by Step
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice** in your WordPress admin.
2. Click the **Products** tab in the left sidebar.
3. Click the **Add New Product** button.
4. Enter a **Product Name** for your bundle (e.g., "Complete Marketing Mastery Bundle").
5. Optionally, add a **Product Description** to explain what's included.
6. Click **Save** to create the product.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Adding Courses to the Bundle
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once your product is created, you need to associate your courses with it:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Open the product you just created by clicking on it in the **Products** list.
2. Look for the **Course Access** or **Content** section within the product settings.
3. Click **Add Course** and select the first course you want to include.
4. Repeat for each additional course you want in the bundle.
5. Click **Save** to confirm your selections.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** You can also add pages, posts, and other custom content to the same product—not just courses. This is useful for including bonus materials, resource libraries, or exclusive blog content alongside your courses.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Configuring Access Rules
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
After adding courses to your bundle, you need to set up access rules so that only students who purchase the product can access the bundled content:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Within the product settings, navigate to the **Access Requirements** section.
2. Configure how students gain access—through a direct purchase, a membership, or a manual grant.
3. Connect your product to a payment gateway if you are selling the bundle (e.g., link to a ThriveCart checkout or a WooCommerce product).
4. Set any additional restrictions, such as time-limited access or drip-released content.
5. Click **Save** to apply the access rules.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** When multiple access rules apply to the same piece of content, Thrive Apprentice uses a content protection priority system. The most restrictive rule takes precedence, so review your rules carefully to ensure students get the access they expect.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully created a course bundle in Thrive Apprentice, added courses to it, and configured the access rules that control who can unlock the bundled content.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
