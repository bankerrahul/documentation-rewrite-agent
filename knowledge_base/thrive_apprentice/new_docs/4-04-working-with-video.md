# How to Work with Video in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to add video descriptions to your courses and lessons, move video descriptions from their default placement on a template, require students to watch a video before marking a lesson complete, and track video events for analytics.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Video is one of the most powerful tools for delivering course content. Thrive Apprentice gives you fine-grained control over how videos appear on your pages, how they interact with lesson progress, and how you can monitor student engagement with video content.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Adding a Video Description to a Course
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
A video description allows you to display a video on your Course Overview page—directly below the course title—giving prospective students an immediate visual introduction to your course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Open the course you want to edit.
3. Click on the **Course Details** tab.
4. Scroll to the bottom of the page and locate the **Video Description** option.
5. Enable the video description toggle.
6. Select or paste the video URL you want to display.
7. Save your changes.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once enabled, the video will appear on your **Course Overview** template, positioned below the course title. You can include both a video description and a regular text description simultaneously—they complement each other on the page.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** A short, engaging video introduction can significantly increase enrollment rates. Use it to give students a preview of what they will learn and why the course matters.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Moving the Video Description Placement
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
By default, the video description appears below the course title on the Course Overview template. If you want to reposition it—for example, placing it in a sidebar or further down the page—you can do so through the template editor.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to your course and preview it on the front end.
2. You will see two editing options:
   - **Edit with Thrive Apprentice Editor** — Modifies the template for all courses using this design.
   - **Edit with Thrive Architect** — Modifies the layout for this specific course only.
3. Choose the appropriate editor based on whether you want to make a global change or a per-course adjustment.
4. Locate the video description element on the template.
5. Drag it to your desired position on the page.
6. Save your changes.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** If you edit the template through the Thrive Apprentice Editor, the change will apply to every course that uses that template. If you only want to move the video for one specific course, use Thrive Architect instead.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Requiring Students to Watch a Video Before Completing a Lesson
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The **Video Watch Progress** feature lets you prevent students from marking a lesson as complete until they have watched a specified percentage of the lesson video. This ensures genuine engagement with your content.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Enabling Video Watch Progress for a New Lesson
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Open the course and create a new lesson.
3. Add a video to the lesson.
4. In the lesson settings, locate the **Video Watch Progress** option.
5. Enable it and set the required watch percentage (e.g., 90%).
6. Save the lesson.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Enabling Video Watch Progress for an Existing Lesson
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Open the existing lesson you want to update.
2. Navigate to the lesson settings.
3. Locate the **Video Watch Progress** option.
4. Enable it and configure the required watch percentage.
5. Save your changes.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once enabled, students will not be able to click the **Mark as Complete** button until they have watched the required amount of the video. This helps maintain the integrity of your course completion metrics.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** Make sure your video is properly embedded in the lesson before enabling this feature. The watch progress tracking relies on the video player being correctly configured within Thrive Apprentice.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Recording Video Events for Analytics
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The **Record Video Events** feature allows you to track how students interact with videos in your lessons. This data is valuable for understanding engagement patterns, identifying drop-off points, and improving your content.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Open the lesson where you want to track video events.
3. In the lesson settings, locate the **Record Video Events** option.
4. Enable it.
5. Save your changes.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once activated, Thrive Apprentice will begin recording video interactions—such as plays, pauses, and completion events—for that lesson. You can view this data in the **Reporting** section of your Thrive Apprentice dashboard to track student progress and engagement.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Combine video event recording with the Video Watch Progress feature for a comprehensive view of how students engage with your video content. Use the reporting data to identify lessons where students tend to drop off and refine your content accordingly.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
