# How to Refresh Templates in Thrive Apprentice After Adding Assessments
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn why you need to refresh your Thrive Apprentice templates after adding assessments, which templates require updating, and how to complete the process in just a few clicks.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Why Templates Need Refreshing
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If you created and designed your Thrive Apprentice course before enabling assessments, your existing templates don't include the layout elements needed to display assessment content. The lesson list template, for example, won't show assessment items, and the assessment template itself won't exist yet in your design.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Refreshing your templates adds the necessary data structures and layout components so that assessments appear correctly on the front end of your site.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** If you're building a brand-new course and enabling assessments from the start, you can skip this step. The templates will already include assessment support when you first design them.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## When You'll See the Refresh Notice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
After you enable assessments for a lesson in an existing course, Thrive Apprentice will display a notice prompting you to reload your templates. You may see this notice in the course editor or when viewing the course on the front end. The message typically asks you to "reload the lesson list template" to reflect the newly added assessment content.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Don't ignore this notice—until you refresh, assessments won't appear for your students even though they're enabled in the backend.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Which Templates Need Refreshing
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Two templates require updates:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Lesson List Template:** This template displays the list of lessons within a course or module. After adding assessments, it needs to be refreshed so that assessment items appear alongside regular lessons in the course structure.
- **Assessment Template:** This template controls how the assessment submission area looks on the lesson page. Refreshing creates this template if it doesn't already exist in your design.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## How to Refresh Your Templates
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Open the course where you've added assessments.
3. Look for the template refresh notice at the top of the page or in the course editor.
4. Click the **Refresh Templates** button (or **Reload Lesson List Template**, depending on the notice).
5. Wait a few seconds for Thrive Apprentice to update the templates.
6. Once complete, you'll see a confirmation that the templates have been refreshed.
7. Preview your course on the front end to verify that assessments now appear correctly in the lesson list and on individual lesson pages.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** Refreshing templates does not delete your existing design or content. It adds the assessment-related elements to your current template structure. Your lesson layouts, styling, and other customizations remain intact.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## What Changes After Refreshing
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
After the refresh, you'll notice the following changes:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- Assessment items appear in the lesson list alongside regular lessons.
- The assessment submission area (upload button, link field, or quiz embed) displays on the appropriate lesson pages.
- Students can see their assessment status (Pending, Passed, Failed) in the course overview.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If you've customized your templates extensively using Thrive Architect, review the refreshed templates to make sure the new assessment elements fit your design. You can adjust the layout and styling of assessment components just like any other Thrive Architect element.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Conclusion
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully refreshed your Thrive Apprentice templates to support assessments. This quick step ensures that your students can see and interact with assessments on the front end of your course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
