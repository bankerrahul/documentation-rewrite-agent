# How to Manage Members in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to use the **Members** section in Thrive Apprentice to view member details, track enrollment and progress, remove members, and import existing members from a CSV file.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Accessing the Members Section
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The **Members** section provides a centralized view of everyone who has signed up for or purchased your courses. To access it:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click **Members** in the left sidebar.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You'll see a dashboard listing all your Thrive Apprentice members. This is your command center for managing student access, tracking progress, and maintaining your membership base.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Understanding the Members Dashboard
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The **Members** dashboard gives you a clear overview of your student base. Here's what you can find:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Engagement Summary
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
At the top of the page, the **Engagement Summary** displays key metrics through a set of cards. Use the filtering options to narrow down the data by date range or specific courses. These cards help you quickly assess overall member activity and engagement.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Member List
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Below the summary, the member list shows each individual member with the following details:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Name and email** — The member's identity and contact information.
- **Enrolled products** — Which products (and their associated courses) the member has access to.
- **Progress** — How far the member has advanced through their courses.
- **Status** — Whether the member's access is active or has been revoked.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Use the search bar and filters at the top of the member list to quickly locate specific members by name, email, or product enrollment.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Managing Member Access
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
From the **Members** section, you can perform several key management actions for any individual member:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Grant access** — Add a member to a product so they can access its courses.
- **Revoke access** — Remove a member's access to a specific product without deleting their account.
- **Unlock or lock content** — Control whether specific lessons or modules are available to a member.
- **View progress** — Check how far the member has progressed through their enrolled courses.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
To manage a specific member's access:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Click on the member's name in the member list.
2. Review their enrollment details and progress.
3. Use the available options to grant, revoke, or modify their access.
4. Click **Save** to apply any changes.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Removing Members
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
There are different levels of member removal depending on your needs. You can revoke product access while keeping the user account, or remove the member entirely from your system.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cv5FIGaKZ_Q
<!-- /wp:embed -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Revoking Product Access
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If you only need to remove a member's access to specific courses—without deleting their WordPress account—use the revocation method:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Members** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the member in the list and click their name.
3. Locate the product you want to revoke access for.
4. Click **Revoke Access** next to the product.
5. Confirm the action.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The member's WordPress account remains intact, but they will no longer be able to access the courses in that product.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Completely Removing a Member
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
To fully remove a member from Thrive Apprentice:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to the **Members** section.
2. Find and click the member you want to remove.
3. Select the **Remove** option.
4. Confirm the removal when prompted.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** Removing a member from Thrive Apprentice does not delete their WordPress user account. It only removes their Thrive Apprentice membership data. To delete the WordPress account entirely, go to **Users** in your WordPress admin dashboard.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Importing Existing Members
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If you're migrating from another membership platform, you can import your existing members into Thrive Apprentice using a CSV file. This ensures a smooth transition without requiring each member to re-register.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Step 1: Export Your Member List
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Most membership plugins allow you to export your member database as a **.CSV file**. Export your list from your previous platform, making sure it includes at minimum:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- Email address (required)
- First name
- Last name
- Any relevant membership or product information
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
### Step 2: Import the CSV into Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice** > **Members**.
2. Click the **Import** button at the top of the members list.
3. Upload your **.CSV file**.
4. Map the CSV columns to the corresponding Thrive Apprentice fields (e.g., email, first name, last name).
5. Select which **products** the imported members should have access to.
6. Click **Import** to begin the process.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Thrive Apprentice will create WordPress user accounts for any members who don't already have one and grant them access to the selected products.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** If a member's email address already matches an existing WordPress user, Thrive Apprentice will link the import to that existing account rather than creating a duplicate.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

## Conclusion

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully learned how to access the Members dashboard, manage member access, remove members, and import existing members from a CSV file. With these tools, you can efficiently maintain your student base as your online school grows.
