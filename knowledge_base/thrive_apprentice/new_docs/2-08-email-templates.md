# How to Set Up Thrive Apprentice Email Templates
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to customize the email templates that Thrive Apprentice sends to your students—including welcome emails, course access notifications, and more—so every message matches your brand and delivers the right information.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## What Are Email Templates?
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Thrive Apprentice automatically sends emails to students at key moments—such as when they enroll in a course, receive access to new content, or complete a course. Email templates let you control the content, design, and personalization of these automated messages.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Accessing Email Templates
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Go to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
3. Select **Email Templates** from the settings menu.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Important:** Before you can customize templates, you must configure an email delivery service provider. If you haven't done this yet, Thrive Apprentice will prompt you to select one when you first open the **Email Templates** settings.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Available Email Templates
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Thrive Apprentice includes templates for the most common student communications:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Welcome Email** — Sent when a student first registers or gains access to your platform.
- **Course Access Email** — Sent when a student is granted access to a specific course.
- **Course Completion Email** — Sent when a student finishes all lessons in a course.
- **Certificate Email** — Sent when a student earns a certificate upon course completion.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** The exact templates available may vary depending on your Thrive Apprentice configuration and connected integrations.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Customizing an Email Template
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. On the **Email Templates** screen, click the template you want to edit.
2. Modify the **Subject Line** to reflect your branding or messaging.
3. Edit the **Email Body** using the built-in editor. You can adjust text, formatting, and layout.
4. Insert **Dynamic Tags** (also called merge fields) to personalize each email automatically.
5. Click **Save** when you're finished.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Using Dynamic Tags for Personalization
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Dynamic tags let you insert student-specific or course-specific information into your emails. Common tags include:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Student Name** — Inserts the student's first name or full name.
- **Course Name** — Inserts the name of the relevant course.
- **Course URL** — Adds a direct link to the course.
- **Site Name** — Inserts your website's name.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
To insert a dynamic tag, place your cursor in the email body where you want the tag to appear and select the tag from the available options in the editor toolbar.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Testing Your Emails
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Always test your emails before they go live to make sure everything looks correct.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. After editing a template, click the **Send Test Email** button.
2. Enter an email address where you'd like to receive the test.
3. Check your inbox for the test email and review the content, formatting, and dynamic tag output.
4. Make any necessary adjustments and save again.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Send test emails to multiple email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) to verify that your formatting renders correctly across platforms.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully configured and customized your Thrive Apprentice email templates, ensuring your students receive polished, personalized communications at every stage of their learning journey.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
