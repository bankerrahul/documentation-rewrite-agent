# How to Manage Product Access Expiry in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to configure access expiry settings on your Thrive Apprentice products so that student access automatically expires after a set period. This is essential for subscription-based courses, limited-time promotions, and time-sensitive content offerings.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## What Is Access Expiry?
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Access expiry is a feature that lets you set a time limit on how long a student can access a product's content. Once the expiry period is reached, the student loses access to all courses and content within that product. This is ideal for:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Subscription-based models** — Grant access for a recurring period (e.g., monthly or yearly) that must be renewed.
- **Limited-time offers** — Provide temporary access to a course as part of a promotional deal.
- **Cohort-based learning** — Restrict access to a specific timeframe aligned with a live course schedule.
- **Trial access** — Let users explore your content for a limited period before requiring a purchase.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Enabling Product Access Expiry
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
To turn on access expiry for a product:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice** > **Products**.
2. Click on the product you want to configure.
3. Open the **Access Expiry** tab in the product settings.
4. Toggle the **Enable Access Expiry** option to **On**.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once enabled, you'll see additional configuration options for setting the expiry duration and related behaviors.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Setting Up an Access Expiry Date
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
After enabling access expiry, configure the duration:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Access Expiry** tab, locate the **Expiry Duration** setting.
2. Enter a number and select the time unit—**days**, **weeks**, **months**, or **years**.
3. The expiry countdown begins from the moment a student is granted access to the product.
4. Click **Save** to apply.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** For a 30-day trial, set the duration to 30 days. For an annual subscription, set it to 1 year. The countdown starts individually for each student based on when they received access.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Enabling a Reminder Notice for Users
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can notify students before their access expires so they have time to renew or complete their coursework:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Access Expiry** tab, find the **Reminder Notice** section.
2. Toggle the **Send Reminder Email** option to **On**.
3. Set the timing for the reminder—for example, **7 days** before expiry.
4. Save your changes.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Thrive Apprentice will automatically send an email notification to the student at the specified interval before their access expires.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Editing the Expiry Reminder Email Template
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
To customize the reminder email your students receive:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Reminder Notice** section, click **Edit Email Template**.
2. Modify the subject line, body text, and any dynamic placeholders (such as the student's name, product name, or expiry date).
3. Preview the email to ensure it looks correct.
4. Click **Save** to apply your changes.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** A clear, friendly reminder email reduces confusion and encourages renewals. Include a direct link to your renewal or purchase page whenever possible.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## What Happens When Access Expires
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
When a student's access expires:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- They can no longer view lessons, modules, or other content associated with the product.
- Their progress data is retained—if they regain access later, they pick up where they left off.
- They see the access restriction message you've configured for the product instead of the content.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can also redirect expired users to a specific page:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Access Expiry** tab, find the **Post-Expiry Redirect** setting.
2. Enter the URL of the page you want expired users to see—such as a renewal page, a special offer page, or a re-enrollment form.
3. Click **Save**.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Create a dedicated "Renew Your Access" page with a compelling message and a clear call to action. This turns expiring access into a re-engagement opportunity.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Conclusion
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully learned how to set up and manage product access expiry in Thrive Apprentice. By configuring expiry durations, reminder emails, and post-expiry redirects, you can create time-limited access models that fit your business strategy.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
