# How to Unlock a Lesson for Users Manually in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to manually unlock specific lessons for individual students in Thrive Apprentice—useful for granting exceptions, troubleshooting access issues, or providing bonus content outside of your normal course progression rules.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before you begin, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Thrive Apprentice** installed and activated on your WordPress site.
- At least one published course with enrolled students.
- The student whose lesson you want to unlock must already have an account on your site.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## When to Manually Unlock a Lesson
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Manual lesson unlocking is helpful in situations where the standard conditional access rules don't apply. Common scenarios include:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Granting Exceptions** — A student needs to skip ahead due to prior knowledge or special circumstances.
- **Troubleshooting Access Issues** — A student reports that a lesson is locked when it shouldn't be, and you need to resolve the issue quickly.
- **Providing Bonus Content** — You want to give a specific student access to a premium or restricted lesson as a reward or incentive.
- **Beta Testing** — You're testing new course content and need to give select students early access to specific lessons.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Unlocking a Lesson from the Member Profile
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The most straightforward way to manually unlock a lesson is through the student's member profile.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In your WordPress dashboard, navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click the **Members** tab in the left sidebar.
3. Search for and click on the student's name to open their profile.
4. Locate the course that contains the lesson you want to unlock.
5. Find the specific lesson in the student's course progress view.
6. Click the **Unlock** option next to the locked lesson.
7. Confirm the action when prompted.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The lesson is now immediately accessible to that student. They will be able to view and interact with the lesson content the next time they access the course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** Manually unlocking a lesson for one student does not affect other students. All other students will continue to follow the standard access rules and conditions you've configured for the course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Unlocking a Lesson from the Course View
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can also approach manual unlocking from the course side rather than the member side.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Apprentice** > **Courses**.
2. Open the course that contains the lesson you want to unlock.
3. Click on the specific lesson to view its settings.
4. Look for the student access or enrollment section within the lesson settings.
5. Search for the student by name or email.
6. Click **Unlock** next to the student's entry to grant them access to this lesson.
7. Click **Save** if required.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Important Considerations
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Keep the following in mind when manually unlocking lessons:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Override Scope** — A manual unlock overrides the conditional access rules for that specific lesson and student only. It does not change the course-wide settings or affect other lessons.
- **Progress Tracking** — The student's progress will still be tracked normally. If they skip lessons through a manual unlock, their overall completion percentage may appear inconsistent until they go back and complete the skipped content.
- **Drip Schedules** — If the lesson is part of a drip schedule, manually unlocking it will make it available immediately, bypassing the scheduled release date for that student.
- **Reversibility** — Depending on your setup, you may be able to re-lock a lesson after unlocking it. Check the lesson settings in the student's profile for this option.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** If you find yourself manually unlocking lessons frequently for the same reason, consider adjusting your course's conditional access rules or creating a separate user role with different access permissions. This will save you time and reduce manual intervention.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully learned how to manually unlock lessons for individual students in Thrive Apprentice, giving you the flexibility to handle exceptions and special cases without changing your course structure.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
