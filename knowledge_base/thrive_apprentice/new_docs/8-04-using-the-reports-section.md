# How to Use the Reports Section in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to navigate the reports dashboard in Thrive Apprentice—including viewing course progress reports, enrollment statistics, completion rates, and filtering and exporting your data.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before you begin, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Thrive Apprentice** installed and activated on your WordPress site.
- At least one published course with enrolled students.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Accessing the Reports Section
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In your WordPress dashboard, navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click the **Reports** tab in the left sidebar.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The reports dashboard gives you a centralized view of all student activity across your courses. From here, you can track enrollment trends, monitor progress, and identify where students may be getting stuck or dropping off.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Understanding the Reports Dashboard
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The reports dashboard is organized into several key sections that give you a complete picture of your course performance.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Enrollment Overview** — Displays the total number of students enrolled across all courses, along with trends over time (daily, weekly, or monthly).
- **Course Progress** — Shows how far students have progressed through each course, broken down by lesson or module completion.
- **Completion Rates** — Highlights the percentage of students who have fully completed each course, helping you identify courses with high drop-off.
- **Activity Timeline** — Provides a chronological view of recent student actions, including enrollments, lesson completions, and course completions.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Viewing Course-Specific Reports
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
To drill down into the data for a specific course:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Reports** section, locate the course list or use the course filter dropdown.
2. Select the course you want to analyze.
3. Review the course-specific metrics that appear, including:
   - Total enrolled students for this course.
   - Average progress percentage across all students.
   - Number of students who have completed the course.
   - Lesson-by-lesson completion breakdown.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Pay attention to lessons with a significant drop in completion compared to the previous lesson. This often indicates content that is too difficult, too long, or not engaging enough—giving you a clear signal for where to improve.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Filtering Report Data
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Filters help you narrow down the data to find exactly what you're looking for.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. At the top of the reports dashboard, locate the filter controls.
2. Apply filters based on the available criteria:
   - **Date Range** — Select a start and end date to view data for a specific time period.
   - **Course** — Choose a specific course or view data across all courses.
   - **Status** — Filter by student status, such as enrolled, in progress, or completed.
3. Click **Apply** or the filter will update automatically, depending on the interface.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** Combining multiple filters gives you more precise results. For example, you can view all students who enrolled in a specific course during the last 30 days and are currently in progress.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Exporting Report Data
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can export your reports for external analysis or record-keeping.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to the report view you want to export.
2. Apply any filters to narrow down the data to the set you need.
3. Click the **Export** button (typically located near the top-right of the report).
4. Choose your preferred export format (CSV is the most common).
5. Save the file to your computer.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Exported CSV files can be opened in spreadsheet applications like Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel for further analysis, charting, or integration with other business tools.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Key Metrics to Monitor
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Here are the most important metrics to track regularly:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Enrollment Rate** — How many new students are joining your courses over time.
- **Completion Rate** — The percentage of enrolled students who finish the entire course.
- **Average Progress** — How far the typical student gets before stopping.
- **Lesson Drop-Off Points** — Specific lessons where students tend to stop progressing.
- **Time to Completion** — How long it takes the average student to finish a course.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully learned how to use the Reports section in Thrive Apprentice to monitor your courses, track student progress, and make data-driven decisions about your content.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
