# How to Create a Student Profile Page in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to create a student profile page in Thrive Apprentice—giving your students a dedicated space to view and manage their account information directly on your website.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Why Students Need a Profile Page
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
A student profile page gives your enrolled users a central hub to manage their account. Without one, students have no easy way to update their name, change their email address, or reset their password—leading to more support requests for you and a frustrating experience for them.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
With a profile page in place, students can:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- View their account details at a glance.
- Update their name, email address, and password.
- Feel more connected to your online school as a polished, professional platform.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before you begin, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Thrive Apprentice** installed and activated on your WordPress site.
- **Thrive Architect** installed for building and editing pages.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Understanding the Two Profile Page Types
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Thrive Apprentice supports two types of profile pages, and you'll typically want to create both:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **My Profile Page (Read-Only)** — Displays the student's account information in a view-only format. Students can see their name, email, and other details but cannot make changes on this page.
- **Edit Profile Page (Interactive)** — Includes the **User Profile** element from Thrive Architect, which lets students actively update their information, change their password, and modify their account settings.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Step 1: Create the "My Profile" Page (Read-Only View)
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Pages** > **Add New**.
2. Enter a page title such as "My Profile" or "My Account."
3. Click **Launch Thrive Architect** to open the visual editor.
4. Design the page layout to display the student's information. You can use dynamic text fields to show the logged-in user's name and email address.
5. Keep this page read-only—do not add editable form fields here.
6. Click **Save Work** when you're finished, then click **Done** to return to WordPress.
7. **Publish** the page.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** This read-only page works well as a dashboard-style overview that links to the separate edit page for making changes.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Step 2: Create the "Edit Profile" Page (Interactive)
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Pages** > **Add New**.
2. Enter a page title such as "Edit Profile" or "Account Settings."
3. Click **Launch Thrive Architect** to open the visual editor.
4. From the right sidebar, search for the **User Profile** element and drag it onto the page.
5. The **User Profile** element automatically provides editable fields for the student's name, email, and password. Configure the element settings to match your preferences.
6. Click **Save Work** when you're finished, then click **Done** to return to WordPress.
7. **Publish** the page.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** The **User Profile** element allows your logged-in students to edit their user information, change their email, update their password, and more—all without needing to access the WordPress admin area.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Step 3: Add a Menu Link to the Profile Page
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once both pages are created, you need to make them accessible to your students by adding a link in your site's navigation:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Appearance** > **Menus**.
2. Select the menu you want to edit (typically your primary navigation menu).
3. Under **Pages**, find your "My Profile" or "My Account" page and click **Add to Menu**.
4. Optionally, add the "Edit Profile" page as a sub-menu item beneath the main profile link.
5. Click **Save Menu**.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** You can also add a profile link directly within your Thrive Apprentice course templates or school design—so students see a "My Account" button while browsing courses.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully created a student profile page with both a read-only view and an interactive edit page, and linked them in your site's navigation so students can easily manage their accounts.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
