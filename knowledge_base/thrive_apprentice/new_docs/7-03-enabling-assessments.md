# How to Enable Assessments in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to enable and configure assessments in Thrive Apprentice. Assessments let you evaluate student work beyond multiple-choice quizzes—students can upload files, submit video links, share external URLs, or complete traditional quiz-based evaluations.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## What Are Assessments?
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Assessments are a feature introduced in Thrive Apprentice version 5.8 that gives you flexible ways to evaluate student progress. Unlike quizzes that are automatically graded, assessments support manual review by the instructor—making them ideal for essays, projects, presentations, and other hands-on assignments.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Thrive Apprentice supports four assessment submission types:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Quiz:** Traditional quiz-based assessments using Thrive Quiz Builder with automated grading.
- **Upload:** Students submit files such as documents, PDFs, presentations, or images.
- **YouTube Link:** Students provide a YouTube video URL—perfect for video presentations, demonstrations, or recorded assignments.
- **External Link:** Students share a URL to work hosted on another platform (e.g., Google Docs, portfolio sites, or code repositories).
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before enabling assessments, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- Thrive Apprentice version 5.8 or later installed and activated
- At least one published course with lessons
- For upload-type assessments, an API connection to Google Drive or Dropbox (see the [assessment upload settings](7-04-assessment-upload-settings.md) guide)
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## How to Enable Assessments for a Course
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click **Courses** in the left sidebar and select the course you want to add assessments to.
3. Open the lesson or module where you want to add an assessment.
4. In the lesson editor, locate the **Assessment** section.
5. Toggle the **Enable Assessment** switch to turn it on.
6. Select the **Assessment Type** from the dropdown:
   - **Quiz** — link an existing Thrive Quiz Builder quiz
   - **Upload** — allow file submissions
   - **YouTube Link** — accept YouTube video URLs
   - **External Link** — accept external URLs
7. Configure any additional settings specific to your chosen type (e.g., passing score for quizzes, allowed file types for uploads).
8. Click **Save** to apply your changes.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** You can add assessments to individual lessons, modules, or as a final course-level evaluation. The placement depends on how you want to structure your grading.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## How Students Submit Assessments
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Once assessments are enabled, students will see a submission area within the lesson page. The submission process depends on the assessment type:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. The student opens the lesson that contains the assessment.
2. They scroll to the **Assessment** section at the bottom of the lesson content.
3. Depending on the type, they either:
   - Complete the embedded quiz
   - Upload a file using the **Upload** button
   - Paste a YouTube video URL into the provided field
   - Enter an external link in the URL field
4. They click **Submit Assessment** to send their work for review.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
After submission, the student sees a confirmation message. They can view their submission status—**Pending**, **Passed**, or **Failed**—from their course dashboard.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Instructor Review Workflow
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
As the instructor, you review and grade non-quiz assessments manually. Here's how:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click **Assessments** in the left sidebar to see all pending submissions.
3. Select a submission to review the student's work.
4. Review the uploaded file, video, or linked content.
5. Choose a result: **Pass** or **Fail**.
6. Optionally, add feedback comments for the student.
7. Click **Save** to finalize the grade.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
The student will be notified of the result, and their course progress will update accordingly. If the assessment is required for lesson completion, the student won't be able to proceed until they receive a passing grade.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** Review assessments regularly to keep students moving through your course. Delayed reviews can frustrate learners and lead to drop-offs.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Conclusion
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully learned how to enable assessments in Thrive Apprentice. With four flexible submission types and a built-in review workflow, assessments give you the tools to evaluate real student work—not just quiz answers.
