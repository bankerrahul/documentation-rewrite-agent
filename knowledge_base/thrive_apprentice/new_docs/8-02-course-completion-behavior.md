# How to Set Up Course Completion Behavior in Thrive Apprentice
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
In this article, you'll learn how to configure what happens when a student completes a course in Thrive Apprentice—including redirect options, completion messages, certificate display, and next course suggestions.

## Prerequisites
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Before you begin, make sure you have:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Thrive Apprentice** installed and activated on your WordPress site.
- At least one published course with lessons that students can complete.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Understanding Course Completion Behavior
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Course completion behavior controls the experience a student has after they finish the final lesson of a course. By default, students remain on the last lesson page. However, you can customize this behavior to create a more engaging and guided post-completion experience.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Accessing Completion Settings
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In your WordPress dashboard, navigate to **Thrive Dashboard** > **Thrive Apprentice**.
2. Click the **Courses** tab in the left sidebar.
3. Open the course you want to configure by clicking on it.
4. Locate the **Completion** section in the course settings panel.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Configuring Redirect Options
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can choose where students are sent after they complete the final lesson.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
- **Stay on Last Lesson** — The student remains on the final lesson page with no automatic redirect. This is the default behavior.
- **Redirect to a Specific URL** — Sends the student to a custom page of your choosing, such as a thank-you page, an upsell page, or a community forum.
- **Redirect to Course Overview** — Takes the student back to the main course page where they can review their progress or explore additional content.
- **Redirect to Next Course** — Automatically directs the student to the next course in a learning path or sequence, helping guide them through a structured curriculum.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
To set a redirect:
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Completion** section, select your preferred redirect option.
2. If you chose **Redirect to a Specific URL**, enter the destination URL in the field that appears.
3. If you chose **Redirect to Next Course**, select the target course from the dropdown.
4. Click **Save** to apply your settings.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** A custom completion page with a congratulations message, certificate download button, and a call to action for your next course creates a polished student experience.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Setting Up a Completion Message
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
You can display a custom completion message to students when they finish the course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Completion** section, look for the **Completion Message** option.
2. Toggle it on to enable a message overlay or notification.
3. Enter your congratulations text. For example: "Congratulations! You've completed the course. Download your certificate below."
4. Customize the appearance and style of the message if options are available.
5. Click **Save** to apply.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Note:** The completion message appears once when the student finishes the last required lesson. It does not reappear if the student revisits the course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Displaying the Certificate on Completion
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
If you've assigned a certificate template to the course, you can configure it to appear automatically when the student completes the course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. Make sure a certificate template is assigned to the course (see [How to Issue Course Completion Certificates](8-01-certificates.md)).
2. In the **Completion** section, enable the option to display the certificate or include a download link in the completion message.
3. Click **Save** to apply.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Students will see their certificate or a download button as part of the completion experience, making it easy to claim their achievement immediately.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
## Suggesting the Next Course
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
Guiding students to their next course keeps them engaged and moving forward in their learning journey.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
1. In the **Completion** section, look for the **Next Course** option.
2. Select the course you want to recommend from the dropdown list.
3. Optionally, customize the call-to-action text that appears alongside the suggestion (e.g., "Ready for the next step? Start Course 2!").
4. Click **Save** to apply.
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
**Tip:** If you have a multi-course learning path, set up each course's completion behavior to point to the next course in the sequence. This creates a seamless progression for your students.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
That's it! You've successfully configured course completion behavior in Thrive Apprentice, giving your students a clear and engaging experience after finishing a course.
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
