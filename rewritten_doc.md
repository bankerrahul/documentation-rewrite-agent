# How to Manage Quiz Statistics in Thrive Quiz Builder

In this article, you'll learn how to manage your quiz statistics settings in Thrive Quiz Builder to optimize your database performance.

## Quiz Stats and Data Settings

To help you control your database size and website performance, **Thrive Quiz Builder** allows you to disable statistics collection or automatically clear old data. This is especially helpful for high-traffic websites.

You can find these options by navigating to **Thrive Quiz Builder** dashboard, clicking on **Settings**, and looking for the **Quiz stats and data** section.

### Enable Stats Tracking

The **Enable stats tracking for all quizzes** option allows you to globally toggle statistics collection on or off.

* **On:** Quiz statistics will be collected for all quizzes. This is the default setting.
* **Off:** No new statistics will be recorded for any quiz.

> [!NOTE]
> This is a global setting that affects **ALL** quizzes on your site.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Disabling tracking does **NOT** delete existing data immediately; it simply stops collecting new data.

### Automatically Clear Old Stats

If you have stats tracking enabled, you will see an option to **Automatically clear old stats**.

This setting allows you to define a retention period for your data (e.g., maintain data for 30 days). Any data older than the specified period will be automatically deleted to save space on your server.

> [!TIP]
> Use this feature to keep your database size manageable without completely sacrificing recent analytics.

That's it! You've successfully configured your quiz statistics settings in Thrive Quiz Builder.
